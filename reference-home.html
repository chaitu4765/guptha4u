Quick answers

Common questions

What is er4u?

er4u (Easy Retail For You) is India's first billing software integrated with an eCommerce website & app — a GST-compliant retail ERP with barcode POS, inventory, loyalty and multi-store management, used by 20,000+ retail, wholesale and manufacturing businesses across the globe. It has offices in Indore, Bhopal and Singapore.

How much does er4u cost?

er4u online plans start at ₹7,000+18% GST per year (Startup, 3 users) and offline plans at ₹5,000+18% GST per year, with a limited-time 30% yearly-plan discount. Lifetime plans with 10-year validity start at ₹11,000+GST (offline) and ₹15,000+GST (online). The Enterprise Max plan at ₹21,000+GST/year already includes a branded eCommerce website; Enterprise Plus at ₹30,100+GST/year adds the branded eCommerce Android app.

Which businesses can use er4u?

er4u serves garment and apparel stores, footwear, supermarkets and kirana, hypermarkets, mobile phone shops, auto parts, book stores, electronics, furniture, opticals, pharmacy & surgical, toys, sports & fitness, boutiques, saree shops and many more retail and wholesale businesses.

Does er4u work offline?

Yes. er4u has 5 online plans (Startup, Premium, Enterprise, Enterprise Max, Enterprise Plus) and 5 offline plans (Startup Off, Premium Off, Enterprise Off, Enterprise Pro, Enterprise Multi) that run on your own PC, so billing never stops when the internet does.
