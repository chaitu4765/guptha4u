# Guptha Arts ERP
Platform: web, locally hosted with Python 3.11 and SQLite.
Audience: Guptha Arts owner and counter operator; shoppers use the storefront.
Confirmed purpose: billing, inventory, customers, orders and ecommerce without a subscription gate.
Reference: er4u public feature descriptions and visual identity. Internal ERP source and UI are unavailable; implementation is independent.
Business: user calls it Guptha Arts; supplied Maps link resolves to Guptas Art Store in Visakhapatnam. Address, GSTIN and catalog require owner confirmation.
Scope: owner authentication, products, customers, suppliers, purchasing, POS, held carts, stock adjustments, order fulfilment/cancellation, printable invoices, storefront COD/pickup orders, reports, CSV export and database backup.
External payment capture, GST filing/e-invoice submission, courier booking and messaging require separate providers and credentials. Recording a payment is not processing it.
User requested 12 clearly labelled demo products with illustrative prices and stock. No demo owner credentials or sales are created.
Confirmed counter workflows: a single invoice can have cash/UPI/card split payments with a balance due; privilege card number applies an owner-configured percentage before tax. Cards support expiry and deactivation.
